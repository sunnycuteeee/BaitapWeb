Welcome to Green Life Club

Welcome to Green Life Club, a friendly community for students who care about nature, sustainability and making a positive difference in our community.

We organize activities where members can learn new skills, meet new friends and work together to create a greener future.

What We Do

Our club organizes different activities throughout the year:

  • Tree planting and environmental campaigns
  • Recycling and clean-up activities
  • Workshops about sustainable living
  • Outdoor trips and nature activities
  • Community volunteering projects

Why Join Us?

  • Make Friends: Meet students with similar interests.
  • Learn New Skills: Join workshops and practical activities.
  • Make an Impact: Take part in projects that help the community.
