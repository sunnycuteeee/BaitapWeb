About Us

Green Life Club is a student club that encourages young people to care about the environment and become active members of their community.

Our goal is to create a friendly environment where students can learn, share ideas and participate in meaningful activities.

Our Mission

Our mission is to inspire students to live more sustainably and work together to build a cleaner and healthier community.

Our Activities

  • Environmental workshops
  • Community volunteering
  • Recycling campaigns
  • Outdoor activities
